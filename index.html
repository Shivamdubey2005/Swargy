A few questions, answered

How is my UPI payment confirmed?

After payment, submit the 12-digit UTR from your UPI app. The administrator independently checks the receiving bank statement. A submitted reference is not a confirmed payment.

Is urgent delivery guaranteed?

No. Confirm serviceability, stock and delivery timing with the store before payment. Live GPS and automatic delivery assignments are not implemented.

Why is the catalog empty?

Only real products added by the administrator are displayed. Illustrative sample products are no longer available for purchase.

Can I pay with cards or receive an automatic refund?

This release supports manual direct UPI transfers only. Card checkout, gateway verification and automated refunds require a separate payment gateway integration.
